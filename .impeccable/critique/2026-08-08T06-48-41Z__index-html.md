## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Does this look AI-generated? No.** It reads as authored. Hand-drawn SVG rooms with per-scene lighting, prose with real voice ("the dust lies wrong on three of them"), and a palette anchored to a near-black desk with a lamp vignette — paper appears only where paper belongs (folders, index cards, cork, rubber stamps). That is an earned dossier identity, not the saturated cream-AI default.

Tells that are present, worst first:
- **Side-stripe borders — 4 confirmed.** `css/style.css:1091` (`.clue-mini`), `:1152` (`.dlg-climax`), `:1330` (`.toast`, 4px), `:1639` (RTL mirror). The toast one is the literal pattern the rule targets, and it never flips for RTL (`border-left` with no logical property), so in Persian the accent sits on the trailing edge.
- **Eyebrow density.** Eleven distinct uppercase tracked micro-label classes. Diegetically defensible in a dossier; at `.56rem` several are decoration pretending to be labels.
- **I / II / III step scaffolding** on the accusation, where the questions already sequence themselves.
- **Orphan grids.** `.dossier-shelf` renders 3+1 at 1440px; `.acc-suspect` renders 3+2 for five suspects.

No gradient text, no glassmorphism, no hero-metric row, and — measured — **zero horizontal overflow** at any route or width.

**Deterministic scan — the mandated command is vacuous, and B proved it.** `detect.mjs --json index.html` and `... index.html js/game.js` both exit 0 with `[]`. B built a control: a page with `border-left: 4px`, `font-family: Inter` and a bounce curve in a *linked* stylesheet returns 0 findings; the same CSS scanned directly or inlined returns 3. `<link rel="stylesheet">` is never followed, so all 1,663 lines of `css/style.css` were skipped by the sanctioned scan. Pointed at the stylesheet directly it found the 4 side-tab hits above. Run against the live app (URL mode, 5 routes) it produced 93 findings, all severity "warning", all `line: 0`.

Most of those 93 are false positives, and B verified them rather than guessing: the 6 Persian "low-contrast" hits measure **13.06:1** when probed directly (the gradient+alpha resolver mis-sampled); `#55493a on #55493a` and `#2b2620 on #000000` are sampling artifacts through the transparent string layer; `nested-cards ×21` fires on a corkboard, where nested cards *are* the subject matter; `dark-glow` fires on the desk lamp the whole conceit is built on.

**Visual overlays: none.** No mutating browser tool is exposed in this session, so `detect.js` was never injected and no user-visible overlay exists. Fallback signal only — headless screenshots plus direct measurement. Both temporary servers were stopped and verified down; nothing was written to the project.

## Overall Impression

This is a beautiful, genuinely authored game sitting on an interaction layer that hasn't caught up to it. The writing, the art, and the Persian edition are craft work. But the interface never tells you that anything happened: you spend an irreplaceable resource and get silence, you form a deduction and get a truncated toast behind a modal, and you end the case forever with an unguarded click.

The single biggest opportunity: **make the game acknowledge the player.** Time spent, evidence found, deductions formed, and the weight of the accusation are four moments where the interface currently says nothing, and all four are the moments the design is actually about.

## What's Working

**The Persian edition is better i18n than most commercial software ships.** Not a translation layer — a second edition. Mirrored stamps and rules, Persian numerals, Persian exhibit letters (الف/ب/پ), a separate font stack with its own size and leading, and the detail that proves someone thought: `[dir="rtl"] #map { direction: ltr }` with `.room { direction: rtl }`, so the floor plan keeps its geography while room contents flip. `localizeCase()` merges field-by-field, so a partial translation degrades instead of crashing.

**Progressive disclosure of the mystery.** A locked hotspot doesn't say "locked" — it says *"The center drawer is locked — a small brass keyhole. Edmund would have kept that key close."* Gated topics announce their existence without revealing themselves. Unformed deductions read "1 of 2 threads in hand. Keep pulling." The player always knows more exists and never knows what.

**Costs are priced in-world before commitment.** `search · 20m`, `question · 10m`, `10m` on each room — diegetic, no tutorial needed. It is the reason the game is playable at all despite having no help screen.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Unguarded `localStorage` silently bricks the game in private mode**
- *What*: `saveGame()` (`game.js:148`), `clearSave()` (`:153`) and `setResult()` (`:162`) write without try/catch, while `setLang`/`loadGame` are guarded.
- *Why it matters*: In Safari private mode or with storage full, the throw inside `spend()` aborts `searchHotspot()` before `grantClues`, `renderLocation` and `showDiscovery` run. Clicking a hotspot does nothing. Forever. No error, no recovery.
- *Fix*: Wrap all three in try/catch matching the existing guarded pattern; on failure, surface one toast ("Progress can't be saved in this browser") and let play continue in memory.
- *Command*: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The irreversible accusation has no guard, no counter, and no confirmation**
- *What*: `#acc-final` renders permanently enabled (`game.js:928`); `finalBtn` is captured only to attach a click handler. One click → `finishCase()` → `G.over = true; clearSave()`. Zero exhibits required. "Choose up to three exhibits" appears above 16 chips with no "2 of 3 chosen" readout, and `.acc-clue` toggles carry no `aria-pressed`.
- *Why it matters*: The highest-stakes action in the product is less protected than deleting a file, and it discards 20+ minutes of play. It also undercuts the game's own writing — the epilogue assumes you meant it.
- *Fix*: Disable until at least one exhibit is chosen, add a live "n of 3" counter, and insert a charge sheet in the case-file voice ("You will charge Vivian Blackwood with the murder of Edmund Blackwood, on the strength of Exhibits A, G and P. This closes the file.") with *Sign the warrant* / *Go back*. Reuse `.verdict-sheet` styling so it reads as a document, not a browser confirm.
- *Command*: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Every escape route renders at 1.73:1**
- *What*: `.btn-quiet` is a dark-surface token (`--kraft #C9AE7E`, 8.35:1 on `--desk`) reused on every paper surface: `Back`, `Not yet — keep digging`, `Done`, `Enlarge photograph`, `Reopen this case`, and all four scene-zoom controls. Measured: **1.73:1 on `--paper`, 1.46:1 on `--paper-dim`**.
- *Why it matters*: A dozen controls fail AA, and the failure is systematic in the worst possible direction — in a timed, irreversible game, *every way out* is the least visible thing on screen.
- *Fix*: Scope an on-paper variant using the project's own proven on-paper ink `#6b5636` (measured **5.66:1** on paper, **4.78:1** on paper-dim) with `border-color: rgba(85,73,58,.45)`. The same idea already exists for `:focus-visible` at `style.css:67–74` — extend it to fill and border.
- *Command*: `/impeccable audit`

**[P1] First run teaches nothing**
- *What*: At 1440×900 the fresh game screen gives the right 42% to an empty cream panel with one italic line, while the labeled, priced hotspot list sits below the fold behind a 10px dark-on-dark scrollbar. No help control exists anywhere. No legend for red vs green vs dashed pins, the `• • •` room dots, or the `VB`/`MH` chips — all of which live only in `title=`. Nothing ever states that clicking costs time.
- *Why it matters*: The opening move is staring at a dark picture with three unlabeled dots beside an empty box. The two mechanics the whole game rests on — pins are clickable, everything costs minutes — are communicated by a `.72rem` caption and cost labels the player hasn't scrolled to.
- *Fix*: Make the empty Evidence panel teach (a first-run card in the file's voice plus a pin/dot/chip legend); lift the hotspot list above the fold or collapse the map to a strip until the first search; and on the first search, animate the clock with a `−20m` delta so the cost registers once, viscerally.
- *Command*: `/impeccable onboard`

**[P2] The corkboard showpiece is inert, and the scene checkmarks float**
- *What*: Two defects. (a) `svg.string-layer` sits at `z-index: 1` beneath the packed `flex-wrap` pin field at `z-index: 2`, so the red strings are occluded for nearly their whole run, and `.corkboard { overflow: hidden }` clips them at the edge; nothing animates when a deduction forms. (b) `.scene-pin-dot` has **no `position: relative`**, so its `::before` checkmark (`inset: 0`) resolves against the flex row instead of the dot — examined checkmarks render ~60px away, floating loose in the illustration.
- *Why it matters*: (a) The board is sold as where deduction becomes visible and is the one view where the reasoning is *less* legible than the plain list — and it's the third tab, so most players never watch a string get tied. (b) makes the best asset in the game look broken.
- *Fix*: (b) is one line: `.scene-pin-dot { position: relative }`. (a) cluster each deduction's source slips near its card so strings cross open cork, animate the string in with `stroke-dasharray`/`stroke-dashoffset`, and make the deduction *arrive* — flash the tab or switch to it — instead of a truncated 3.8s toast behind a modal.
- *Command*: `/impeccable animate`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (Confused First-Timer)** — Presses "Begin the Investigation" and the pinned note *"the master alone drinks from the private Reserve. Start there"* — the literal first move — is destroyed; `renderBriefing()` is unreachable from the game screen. Lands on a screen whose largest element is empty. Sees `• • •` and `VB` with meanings hidden in `title=`. Clicks a red dot because it looks clickable; 20 minutes vanish with no acknowledgement. Presses the big red "Make an Accusation" out of curiosity — step 3 has no visible cancel and the "Back" is a 1.73:1 ghost, so one more click ends his first case permanently. Looks for help; there is none anywhere in `index.html`, `game.js` or `i18n.js`. **Abandons at the first empty screen.**

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User)** — 390px: the deadline countdown is `display: none`, so she cannot tell she's running out of night. Scene pins shrink to a measured **26×26** and lose their labels, while every other control is guaranteed 44px — `.scene-pin` is the one interactive class missing from the coarse-pointer min-height list, and it's the primary interaction. Tapping one is a blind 20-minute spend. The journal stacks below the entire board pane, so reaching evidence means scrolling past map, illustration, description and person list every time. On accusation step 3 the primary button sits below all 16 chips, off-screen. Credit: `saveGame()` fires on every spend, so an interruption doesn't lose progress.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent)** — A dozen secondary controls at 1.73:1. Suspect names, room-dot counts and corkboard clue descriptions exist only as `title=` — unreachable by keyboard, unreliable in screen readers, invisible on touch. `.acc-clue` toggles expose no `aria-pressed`, so a screen-reader user cannot tell which of 16 exhibits are selected. Every hotspot is a redundant tab stop (once as pin, once as row) with different labels for the same action. `.room.here` stays focusable with a handler that early-returns and no `aria-disabled`. Credit where earned, and measured: **0 unnamed interactive elements** across all routes, correct `role="tab"`/`aria-selected` on 3 of 3 tabs, 2 `aria-live` regions, a real focus ring that resolves at 7.93:1 on desk and 4.56:1 on paper, a properly built modal focus trap, and `prefers-reduced-motion` fully honored (`style.css:1442` neutralizes both keyframes and all 19 transitions). Someone thought about Sam; they just didn't finish.

## Minor Observations

- **The debug hash ships in production.** `bootFromHash()` is live: anyone typing `#case:blackwood,demo` gets every clue and deduction of a murder mystery revealed — a spoiler backdoor in a game whose entire value is not knowing. It also sets `DEBUG = true`, silently disabling saves. Gate it behind localhost or a build flag.
- **`.lang-toggle` breaks Persian glyph joining** — measured `letter-spacing: 0.7488px` on `فارسی` in a Latin monospace stack severs the cursive joins ("فا رسی"). It renders Persian while in English mode, so no `.lang-fa` override can fix it; it needs its own rule.
- **Gradient-endpoint contrast**: `#453927` on the kraft folder measures 5.27:1 at the light end but **3.42:1 at the dark end** (`style.css:293, 441, 456`). `.empty-note` composites to 4.93:1 on paper but **4.33:1** on paper-dim. Token-level review can't see either.
- **`؟ ؟ ؟`** hardcoded at `game.js:828` shows an Arabic question mark to English players.
- **Two tabs = silent progress loss**; both write `SAVE_KEY`, last write wins.
- **The language toggle exists only on the title screen** — a Persian speaker landing mid-session cannot switch.
- **Persons tab is a dead end** — five well-written cards, none clickable, no way to travel to the person.
- **"The Board" doesn't say what it is**; "Deductions (2)" would, and the count already exists.
- **Multiple deduction toasts can stack**, each truncated at 107 chars, each 3.8s, unqueued, behind an open modal.
- **Google Fonts is a hard external dependency** — offline, the entire typographic identity collapses to Georgia/Courier/Tahoma.
- **Custom scrollbars** at 10px dark-on-dark are the only cue that the location panel scrolls, which is why the hotspot list goes undiscovered.

## Questions to Consider

1. **The corkboard is your thesis and your third tab.** The Evidence list is a strictly worse view of the same data — and it's the default. What if the board *were* the journal, with evidence pinning itself to the cork as it's found and strings drawing in as deductions form, and "Evidence" became a plain index you open only to read a description?

2. **You built an eight-hour clock and then made spending it invisible.** What would this feel like if time were physical — the clock sweeping when you search, the lamplight shifting toward dawn across the case, the last hour tinting the paper? You already own the entire visual language; `.topbar-clock.danger` pulses at 60 minutes and nobody notices.

3. **The accusation is the only moment the player is truly alone, and it's the one moment you don't write for them.** You wrote four epilogues per case. Why is there no charge sheet restating who they're accusing, why, and on what evidence, before the warrant is signed? That page fixes error prevention, working memory, and the emotional valley in one move.

4. **You shipped two editions and hid one of them.** Is the Persian edition a feature you're proud of, or a secret?
